MASTER CLOUD SERVICES & SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Between: CloudPlatform Technologies LLC ("Provider") and Apex Logistics Global ("Customer")

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider agrees to deliver 24/7 managed cloud infrastructure hosting, automated disaster recovery backups, and tier-1 technical support as outlined in Exhibit A.

2. SERVICE LEVEL OBJECTIVES (SLO) & UPTIME
Provider guarantees a monthly system uptime commitment of 99.95% ("Uptime Commitment"), excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.
- In the event monthly uptime falls between 99.0% and 99.95%, Customer shall be credited 10% of monthly billing.
- In the event monthly uptime falls below 99.0%, Customer shall be credited 25% of monthly billing.

3. SECURITY, DATA PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE
Provider shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification, ISO/IEC 27001 compliance, and enforce AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit.
Security incident notifications must be delivered to Customer's designated CISO within 4 hours of confirmed data intrusion.

4. FEES, BILLING & PAYMENT TERMS
Monthly recurring subscription fee: $14,500.00 USD.
Invoices shall be generated on the 1st of each calendar month with Net 30 payment terms.
A late fee of 1.5% per month shall apply to overdue balances.

5. TERMINATION & REMEDIES
Either party may terminate this agreement upon 60 days written notice. Immediate termination is permitted in the event of uncured material breach lasting more than 15 consecutive business days.
Total liability under this agreement is capped at 12 months of paid service fees ($174,000.00 USD).
