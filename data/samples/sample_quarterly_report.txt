EXECUTIVE OPERATIONS & PERFORMANCE REPORT — Q4 FY25
Apex Logistics Global — Infrastructure & Digital Operations Division
Date of Publication: January 15, 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
During Q4 FY25, Apex Logistics expanded its global fulfillment network across 18 regional distribution hubs, achieving a record 98.4% on-time package delivery rate despite severe seasonal weather conditions. Migration to managed cloud architecture reduced unplanned operational downtime by 44%.

KEY PERFORMANCE METRICS
- Total Shipments Processed: 4.85 Million units (+18.2% YoY).
- Cloud Infrastructure Operating Costs: $43,500.00 for Q4 (averaged $14,500.00/month).
- Customer Satisfaction Index (CSAT): 92.6% (+4.1 points over Q3).
- System Uptime Delivered: 99.98% across all core logistics management services.

OPERATIONAL RISKS & CHALLENGES
1. Fuel & Freight Surcharges: Fluctuating maritime diesel prices caused a 3.4% margin compression in cross-border logistics.
2. Warehouse Labor Constraints: Peak holiday volume required temporary contractor surge staffing, increasing variable labor expenditure by $120,000.00.
3. Cybersecurity Alert: Monitored 14 phishing attempts targeting logistics dispatcher credentials; zero breaches occurred due to multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforcement.
